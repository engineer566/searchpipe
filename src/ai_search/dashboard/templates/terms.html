1. Service Description

The Service aggregates results from multiple search engines, fetches and cleans page content, and reranks and summarizes results with large language models, returning structured search results. You may access the Service through the MCP Server (for clients such as Claude Code and Cursor) or through direct API calls.

3. Credits, Recharges and Subscriptions

The Service uses a prepaid credit system with two purchase options: credit recharges and monthly subscriptions. Please read this section carefully and purchase according to your actual needs.

Search requests consume credits per call: a basic search costs 1 credit, and an advanced search costs 2 credits. Credits are non-transferable, cannot be withdrawn as cash, and cannot be exchanged for money. If a billing anomaly is caused by a technical failure on our side, we will resolve it by issuing additional credits rather than cash refunds.

Please note the difference in credit validity: credits obtained through recharges never expire; credits obtained through monthly subscriptions (including renewals and upgrades) are valid for 30 days from the moment they are credited and expire automatically if unused.

(a) Credit Recharges

  • Exchange rate: $0.005 = 1 credit;
  • Fixed tiers: $5 = 1,000 credits, $10 = 2,100 credits, $20 = 4,400 credits;
  • Recharged credits never expire.

(b) Monthly Subscriptions

  • Plans: Starter $4.99/month = 1,200 credits per month; Pro $9.99/month = 3,000 credits per month; Max $19.99/month = 10,000 credits per month;
  • Subscription credits are valid for 30 days from the moment they are credited; unused subscription credits expire automatically;
  • Subscriptions renew automatically each billing cycle until cancelled. You can cancel at any time through the customer portal (linked from the billing page), and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period;
  • Renewal credits take effect at the start of the next billing period and are valid for 30 days; they do not accumulate into the current period;
  • Upgrades: upgrading requires payment of the full current price of the new plan (the remaining value of the old plan is not deducted). The new plan's credits are credited immediately and are valid for 30 days from the moment of upgrade; unused credits from the old plan are extended to expire at the same time as the new plan's credits;
  • Downgrades are not supported; during an active subscription you can only renew or upgrade.

When consuming credits, subscription credits that are closest to expiring are used first; never-expiring recharge credits are used last. Please plan your usage accordingly before purchasing.
