Pricing

SearchPipe uses prepaid credits: ${{ recharge_rate }} = 1 credit — basic search costs 1 credit per call, advanced search 2 credits. Free credits on signup; failed requests are auto-refunded, never charged.

Monthly plans (credits valid 30 days)

Subscription credits are valid for 30 days from grant; unused credits expire at the end of the period. Plans renew automatically via the platform's subscription management:

Plan rules

  • Renewal: renewed credits take effect from the next billing period and do not stack into the current one.
  • Upgrade: pays the full current price of the new plan; new-plan credits land immediately with a fresh 30-day window, and unused credits from the old plan are extended to the new period end.
  • Downgrades are not supported. Subscriptions are platform-managed and renew automatically — cancel anytime from the billing portal.
  • Subscription credits and recharged credits are tracked separately: time-limited batches are consumed first.

Billing & refunds

  • Per-call billing: basic search 1 credit, advanced search 2 credits.
  • Failures are never charged: upstream retrieval failures (502) and output content violations (400) are auto-refunded; retries are safe.
  • Result cache: identical query and parameters within 300s hit the cache and are not billed again.
  • Credit validity and refund policies follow the Terms of Service; service failures on our side are compensated after verification.

Rate limits

Default sliding-window limits: 100 requests per minute with a burst of 20; over-limit requests return 429 with a Retry-After header. Need higher concurrency? Reach out via console feedback.

FAQ

  • Do credits expire? Recharged credits never expire; subscription credits are valid for 30 days. See the FAQ.
  • How do I integrate? See the MCP setup guide and the API docs.
